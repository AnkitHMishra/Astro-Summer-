# 🚀 Welcome to the Launchpad Astrophysics Summer Program

### *For the Sophomores of IIT Bombay* 🌌✨

> *“Somewhere, something incredible is waiting to be known.”* — Carl Sagan

Have you ever looked at the night sky and wondered how black holes consume matter, why neutron stars emit intense X-rays, or how gravity bends space and time itself?
Then welcome — this summer, we begin that journey together. 🌠

This program is designed as an exciting introduction to modern astrophysics, especially for curious second-year students eager to explore physics beyond textbooks. Over the coming weeks, we will dive into the fascinating universe of:

* 🌌 Black Holes & Accretion Physics
* ✨ Neutron Stars & X-ray Binaries
* 📡 High-Energy Radiation Processes
* 🌀 Relativity & Space-Time Curvature
* 🔭 Spectral Analysis and Real Astronomical Data
* 💻 Computational Astrophysics & Simulations

I’m **Ankit Mishra**, currently pursuing an M.Sc. in Physics at Indian Institute of Technology Bombay, with research interests in **high-energy astrophysics**, particularly compact objects, accretion physics, radiative transfer processes, and X-ray spectral modeling. My work involves understanding some of the most extreme environments in the universe using observational and computational techniques. I have also worked on interdisciplinary research projects connecting physics, astronomy, and computational methods. 🔬⚡

This program will not just be lectures — we will actively *do astrophysics*.
You will encounter:

* 🧠 Thought-provoking conceptual challenges
* 📊 Mini research-style assignments
* 🛰️ Real astronomical datasets
* 🧮 Numerical and computational exercises
* 🌟 Exploratory projects designed to build intuition and curiosity

Some assignments may initially look mysterious or difficult — and that is intentional. Astrophysics grows through curiosity, persistence, and asking bold questions. 🚀

So whether you are here because you love the stars, enjoy physics, or simply want to explore something new, I encourage you to participate actively, ask questions fearlessly, and think deeply.

The universe is vast.
Let’s begin exploring it together. 🌌✨
